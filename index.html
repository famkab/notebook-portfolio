Entry 01 · p. 2

Beeline

Greater Manchester’s careers service, from system map to metrics

The MBacc sets out technical and academic routes into work, and it was born in Greater Manchester. Beeline is the young person’s version of it, and I lead its product and service design here, where the policy started: the system map, the online and offline experiences, the evidence loop and the metrics.

  • ScaleRegional · 10 boroughs
  • AudienceYoung people up to 25

Beeline Unplugged

The service as a deck of cards

A deck of cards that does part of what the digital service does, without the digital. Eighteen prompts, a scoring model that shows how a young person’s interests and skills might map to different areas of work, and a tally chart they fill in by hand and take home.
