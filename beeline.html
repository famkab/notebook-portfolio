The short version

Four things, before the detail: what was wrong, what I do about it, where it runs and how we know whether it works.

The problem

Greater Manchester rebuilt its careers offer around the and its seven . Young people were being asked to navigate that system with no clear line between what they enjoy, the subjects they pick, the pathways open to them and a real next step. The people who need it most are the least likely to be reached by a website alone.

What I do

I lead the product and service design. I mapped the whole careers landscape into a problem model, designed the Discovery quiz online and offline, took both through co-design and testing with young people, and run the evidence loop that decides what gets built: with partners, user research with our researcher, for the trade-offs, then the roadmap and developer handoff.

Where it runs

Ten boroughs, six regional events, schools, colleges, youth organisations and community settings, plus a printed toolkit for groups with low digital access or additional needs. The audience is young people up to 25, and the adults around them.
