JUMP Math Lab

Maths modules and a teacher dashboard built on xAPI data. I took the dashboard through three rounds, from counts of activity to something a teacher can act on before the next lesson.

SectorNon-profitScaleInternationalAudienceK–8 teachers and their studentsProductTeacher learning platform

JUMP Math Lab gave teachers a lot of data and very little to act on. I redesigned what the Lab measures and how the teacher dashboard shows it, so a teacher can see who needs help, with what, before the next lesson.

My role: wrote the proposal, designed the dashboards in Figma, and specified the tracking data for developersWhen: 2024–2025Outcome: shipped; teacher feedback was widely positive and fed the next iteration
SectorNon-profitScaleInternationalAudienceK–8 teachers and their studentsProductTeacher learning platform
The problemLots of data, little to act on

Teachers only got a pre- and post-assessment, and only the post was useful. Every student had to attempt the bonus questions, ready or not. Curriculum links lived in static PDFs, kept as two separate lists for Canada and the US.

My roleFrom proposal to shipped screens

I wrote the proposal, designed the student, teacher and admin dashboards in Figma, and specified the tracking data developers needed, splitting every change into needs and wants.

What I didFive changes, one proposal
  • Forked pathways: bonus or scaffolded questions, chosen by each student’s own results
  • Check-in quizzes after every lesson, and all content sorted into diagnostic, formative and summative
  • Short confidence questions built into quizzes
  • A module library that shows curriculum links for your province or state
  • Dashboards that lead with who needs help, and with what
What changedShipped, and teachers liked it

The changes shipped. Teacher feedback was widely positive and fed straight into the next iteration. In the teacher survey that started it, every teacher who answered asked for more quizzes and tests, and check-ins gave them exactly that.

Forked pathways: bonus or scaffold

Before, every student had to attempt two bonus questions. Now each section has five core questions, and the result picks the next step: 1–3 correct leads to two scaffolded questions with hints, 4–5 correct leads to two bonus questions. Tap the questions to change one student’s answers.
