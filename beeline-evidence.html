Pick a fear, pain or barrier to follow

Every Beeline metric climbs the same four steps: Named (it exists), Defined (we agreed what to count), Baselined (we have a first number) and Trended (we can see which way it’s going). A metric only climbs when the evidence allows it, and the ladder shows where each one honestly stands.

One metric, with a first number

LM-03 Decision Quality asks whether young people leave with more options than they arrived with. A session counts as broadening when it explores two or more . This is the first month it could be read, and it reads badly: most sessions narrow to one gateway, and the ones that broaden cluster in Digital and Engineering.

Explored two or more gateways Narrowed to one Paired-gateway sessions, 6 July to 4 August 2026. Percentages rounded to the nearest five.

A metric that comes back with a number you did not want is still the metric working. It named the problem the Discovery Quiz redesign is now aimed at, and it gave the board a baseline to judge the next release against.
